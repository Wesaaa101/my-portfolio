IMPORTANT

6. After uploading, wait for GitHub Pages and Vercel to redeploy, then test both live URLs.
7. Before submitting, run Lighthouse and enter your actual before/after scores in README.md.

This package cannot publish to your accounts automatically. You must upload/commit the files while signed in to GitHub.
